# Ethnic-Thesia
**Developed by Whazorz**

Ethnic-Thesia is a majestic, high-performance desktop visualizer and particle simulation software. It transforms static image data into living, breathing fluid dynamics, driven by mathematics and your system's master volume.

## The Gestaltic Feature: Picture-to-Picture Evolution
At the core of Ethnic-Thesia is the **Gestaltic Particle Evolution** engine. The software does not simply fade or cut between pictures; it *evolves* them.

- **Data Deconstruction:** The original image is shattered into 20,000 independent geometric particles.
- **Fluid Morphing (The Evolution):** Instead of chaotic scattering, the particles seamlessly and geographically morph their fundamental structures from one picture's mathematical data to the next. The fluid organically glides and twists between forms.
- **Gestalt Emergence:** Driven by flawless Sine pendulums, the abstract, unrecognizable fluid ribbons gracefully gather back together into the exact silhouette of the new picture. It beautifully demonstrates the psychological principle of *Gestalt*—where the recognizable whole magically emerges from a storm of abstract parts.

## Core Features
* **Mathematical Nature Flow:** The fluid turbulence is strictly governed by Fibonacci amplitudes (34, 89, 144) and the Golden Ratio (Phi), weaving particles into perfect nautilus-like spirals without relying on artificial noise.
* **PRNG Image Seeding:** Every picture mathematically generates a unique Pseudo-Random Seed based on its specific pixel data. This seeds the physics engine, ensuring every picture has a totally unique, perfectly-fitted fluid evolution flow.
* **Master Volume Spectral Reactivity:** Ethnic-Thesia silently intercepts the Windows Master Volume. Every individual particle is mapped to a specific frequency bin (from deep bass to high treble), causing the fluid to vibrate and dilate as a hyper-detailed audio visualizer.
* **Extreme Performance:** Built on heavily optimized hardware-accelerated Canvas floats, skipping heavy trigonometric rendering when particles are resting to ensure flawless 60fps execution.
